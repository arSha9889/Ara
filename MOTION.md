# AraChat Motion

## Format
- TikTok: 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, 30 seconds.
- Keep headline, logo, and call to action in the central safe area, clear of TikTok's top and bottom overlays.
- The visual story must work without sound.

## Palette
- Canvas: #0A111C.
- Devices and cards: #141F30 and #1A273A.
- Primary text: #F3F7FC.
- Secondary text: #B0BECF.
- Active controls and icons: #4AA8F0.
- Highlights: #8CC9FF.
- Borders: #2A3B53.
- The parrot's violet and yellow-orange colours are reserved for the logo and character.

## Type
- Gilroy.
- Headlines: Bold, 68-92 px.
- Subheadlines: Medium, 34-44 px.
- Labels: Medium, 26-32 px.
- Headlines use two lines or fewer and are left-aligned or centred within each scene.

## Timing and motion
- Headlines and slogans enter with Blur Fade In over 240-360 ms: soft blur and opacity resolve to a sharp, stable wordmark.
- Product screens rise 24-32 px and fade in over 400-500 ms.
- Chat bubbles enter with a 12-18 px Pop-up offset and no bounce. New messages arrive one after another.
- Chat scrolling stays smooth and evenly paced, without abrupt acceleration.
- The Ara parrot is a friendly guide: a small tilt, blink, or gentle appearance from a message bubble. It never distracts from the interface.
- Scene transitions use fades, card shifts, and soft refocusing. No flashes.

## Texture and finish
- Use a calm dark canvas with a soft, diffused blue glow behind the device.
- Present supplied AraChat screens in a vertical Android device frame. Crop the tablet screens only to preserve readable interface details.
- The real interface is the proof. The parrot and abstract chat bubbles are secondary accents.

## Never
1. Sharp flashes or strobing.
2. Neon or acidic accents outside the AraChat palette.
3. Bouncy or overly cartoonish interface motion.
4. Invented screen data, features, or marketing claims.
5. Personal email addresses, AraChat IDs, or other personal data from supplied screens.

## Example scene
On a dark canvas, the headline "Спокойное общение - рядом" resolves through Blur Fade In. A real chat-list screen rises gently into view. The Ara parrot briefly peeks from a small message icon while two neutral chat bubbles appear in sequence. The screen then scrolls smoothly into a search scene.
