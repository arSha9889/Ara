# Brand

## Name and what we do
AraChat is a Messenger for close and calm communication.

## Audience
People aged 16 and over who want a technological and warm messenger for communicating with friends, family, and loved ones.

## What we promote
The native AraChat Android app and its capabilities: personal chats, finding people by AraChat ID or username, audio and video calls, and privacy settings.

Video link: https://aramsg.ru/downloads
Call to action: "Узнать о запуске Android".

## The feeling
Technological, calm, warm.

## Assets
- Logo: an Ara parrot in a message icon, supplied by the user.
- Font: Gilroy.
- Interface: supplied real AraChat Android tablet screens.
- Canvas: #0A111C.
- Cards: #141F30.
- Raised surfaces: #1A273A.
- Primary text: #F3F7FC.
- Secondary text: #B0BECF.
- Accent: #4AA8F0.
- Light accent: #8CC9FF.
- Borders: #2A3B53.

## Never
- Use sharp flashes.
- Invent interfaces, features, or product claims.
- Show personal data from supplied screens.
